Chapter 1

The idea ایده

Marzgah means “the place at the border”. On the old roads of Iran, that place was the caravanserai gate: the point where a guarded border opened for travellers who had the right papers. The panel does the same job for Marzban servers. Admins and resellers keep the border; customers pass through.

مرزmarzborder, frontier
گاه-gāhplace, station
مرزگاهMarzgahthe border post, the gate

Personality

  • Persian ایرانی
  • Trustworthy امین
  • Orderly منظم
  • Open گشوده

A gatehouse, not a hacker tool. Calm, exact and welcoming to people who are allowed in.

Writing the name

Persian: مرزگاه, one word with no space or half-space.
Latin: Marzgah in running text. Use MARZGAH only inside the logo.

Not “MarzGah”, “Marzgaah” or “Marz Gah”.

Chapter 2

The mark نشانه

An iwan, the framed Persian portal, set into a border line. The line runs unbroken except at one place: the doorway.

  • GridBuilt on a 256-unit square in steps of 16. The outer band, inner pier and border line are all 16 units.
  • FrameA 10-unit channel separates the frame from the piers, like the decorated border of a pishtaq, the portal façade.
  • ArchA pointed two-centred arch. Its centres (saffron dots) sit 16 units either side of the axis, with a radius of 58.
  • BorderThe base line runs 216 units wide and is cut only by the 84-unit doorway.
  • OpticsThe mark sits 6 units above the geometric centre, so it looks centred. On dark grounds the channel opens to 11 units to offset light bleed.

Chapter 3

Logo versions نسخه‌ها

Persian comes first. In Persian layouts the gate stands to the right of the wordmark, where reading begins. Use the Latin-first version only where the surrounding text is English.

Stacked

Square spaces such as the login card, splash screens, posters and social posts.

Latin horizontal

English documents, GitHub and the README. The gate leads on the left.

Persian wordmark

Where the gate already appears nearby, such as a page header below the app icon.

Symbol

Avatars, app icon, favicon and stamps. For 24 px and below, use the small cut.

Reversed

Light turquoise mark and white wordmark on Lajvard.

One colour

White on Firuzeh or photos, black for fax, stamps and engraving.

Accessibility

  • Body text and buttons on light grounds use Firuzeh Deep (6.0 : 1 on white). Plain Firuzeh (3.6 : 1) is only for the mark, icons and large display type.
  • On Lajvard, use Firuzeh Light #3FC1B4 (6.6 : 1) or white.
  • Za'feran never carries white text. Put Lajvard text on it, or use it on Lajvard.
  • Never use colour alone to show status. Pair it with a word or an icon, such as منقضی next to Anar.

* Pantone references are the nearest matches. Check them against a physical swatch book before printing.

Persian and RTL rules

  • Use the half-space (ZWNJ) properly: می‌شود، نماینده‌ها، به‌روزرسانی. Never use a full space or join the parts.
  • Use Persian digits (۰۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹) in Persian text, prices and dates. Use Latin digits only in links, tokens, IPs and code.
  • Use the Solar Hijri calendar in Persian interfaces, and set amounts in toman with a thousands separator: ۱۲۰٬۰۰۰ تومان.
  • Never letter-space Persian, and never set it in Poppins or any font that lacks proper Persian joining.
  • Mirror the layout and directional icons in RTL. Don't mirror the logo, the symbol or icons for real objects.

Chapter 7

Pattern & graphic elements نقش

The arcade pattern repeats the doorway across a caravanserai wall. Always use it tone on tone, quiet enough that text can sit on top of it.

Arcade on Kahgel
Arcade on Lajvard
Arcade on Firuzeh

The border line

A 3 px rule broken by a small gate. Use it to divide chapters, as on the pages of this book, and as an empty-state illustration.

Icons

The panel uses Lucide line icons at 1.5–2 px stroke with rounded joins. Keep icons in currentColor and never in Firuzeh outlines next to the logo. The gate is the only filled shape on the screen.

Corners

The mark is square-cornered. Interface radii stay small (8–12 px) so that cards don't fight the hard architecture of the gate.

Chapter 9

Voice لحن

Write like a polite gatekeeper: brief, exact and respectful. Use formal-friendly Persian (شما, not تو). Use no exclamation marks, no scare tactics and no technical jargon with customers.

Sayاشتراک شما ۵ روز دیگر تمام می‌شود. برای تمدید، دکمه‌ی زیر را بزنید.
Don't say!!! هشدار: اکانتت داره منقضی میشه، سریع تمدید کن

Chapter 11

Files فایل‌ها

All artwork is in the repository under brand/. SVG is the master format. Every logo exists in four colourways: color, reversed, black and white.

brand/logo/Horizontal, Latin horizontal, stacked, Persian wordmark, symbol and small symbol as SVG.
brand/png/1200 px transparent PNGs of every logo SVG.
brand/web/favicon.ico, favicon.svg, PNG icons, apple-touch-icon, maskable icon, site.webmanifest and a <head> snippet.
brand/pattern/Arcade pattern tiles in Kahgel, Lajvard and Firuzeh.
brand/presentation/The identity presentation board and slides with mockups.
brand/build_kit.pyRegenerates every file from the geometry. Change the numbers there, never in exported files.
