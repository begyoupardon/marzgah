What we heard

The brief

Marzgah (مرزگاه, 'border post') is the reseller panel for Marzban VPN servers: admins run resellers, resellers serve their customers. The identity should feel Persian, trustworthy and calm — a gatehouse, not a hacker tool.

Persiantrustworthyorderlyopen

Distinct from blue-shield VPN marks, unmistakably Persian without religious symbols, readable at 16 px, works in one colour and RTL.

Concept A

Iwan — the border gate

A Persian iwan portal set into a border line; the line breaks only where the gate opens.

  • Says the name: a gate (gāh) in a border (marz)
  • Caravanserai architecture — Persian, secular, built for travellers
  • Turquoise and lapis from Isfahan's tilework, not VPN blue
